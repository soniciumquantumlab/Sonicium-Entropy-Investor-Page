The Infrastructural Crisis

Cloud Native Entropy Starvation

Virtualized, containerized, and zero-trust service mesh architectures have eliminated the physical interrupts (/dev/random) that traditional kernels rely on. Simultaneous boot storms and high-concurrency mTLS handshakes exhaust host entropy pools, forcing weak PRNG fallbacks or blocking boot sequences entirely.

virtio-rng Saturation

During auto-scaling events, hundreds of containers boot simultaneously. The host virtio-rng interface saturates, causing kernel blocks or predictable seed usage.

mTLS Handshake Load

Istio/Linkerd service meshes perform millions of ephemeral key and nonce generations per second. Software PRNGs introduce latency spikes and duplicate-key risks.

PQC Seed Sensitivity

FIPS 203 (ML-KEM) and FIPS 204 (ML-DSA) are structurally more sensitive to seed bias. Correlated lattice noise enables SVP secret recovery; rejection sampling leaks private keys.

Global PQC Landscape

Recent Developments

Curated signals from the post-quantum cryptography transition — regulatory milestones, standardization, and adoption trends.

Regulation Oct 2026

Vietnam Announces 3 Draft National PQC Standards

Vietnam's Government Cipher Committee published draft TCVN standards for HB-PQSign, ML-KEM, and ML-DSA, targeting 100% quantum-resistant migration of critical systems by 2035. The roadmap explicitly identifies "harvest now, decrypt later" as a present threat, with PQC as the primary solution and QKD reserved for selective critical networks. Feedback from experts and industry is being solicited through official portals.

Source: Vietnam.vn →
Migration Oct 2026

Singapore Sets 2031 Quantum-Safe Deadline for CII

The Cyber Security Agency of Singapore (CSA) issued binding migration guidance: Critical Information Infrastructure systems must complete quantum-safe migration by 2031, with other exposed organizations encouraged to finish by 2035. A S$ quantum-safe kickstarter grant was launched to fund pilot deployments. CSA emphasized that cryptographic discovery, inventory, and legacy OT migration are priority use cases for early adopters.

Source: CSA Singapore →
Standardization May 2026

NIST Advances Nine Candidates for Additional PQC Signatures

NIST selected nine digital signature algorithms — including FAEST, MAYO, SQIsign, and UOV — for the third round of its additional PQC standardization process. The evaluation will last approximately two years, with updates expected before the 7th NIST PQC Conference in 2027. Notably, HAWK was withdrawn after Anthropic's AI model discovered a mathematical vulnerability, underscoring the rigor of cryptographic review in the AI era.

Source: NIST →
Adoption Oct 2026

F5 Labs: Critical Infrastructure Sectors Lag on PQC Readiness

A scan of the top 1 million websites found global PQC adoption at 54%, but critical sectors trail significantly: oil & gas at 34%, telecom at 35%, and government at 35%. The report notes that removing Cloudflare-hosted domains drops global adoption to 22%, revealing deep reliance on edge providers for quantum readiness. 11% of sites still lack TLS 1.3 support entirely, making PQC migration impossible without rearchitecting.

Source: Digitalisation World →
Defense Oct 2026

Airbus Demonstrates Live Quantum-Secure Tactical Network

Airbus Defence and Space successfully demonstrated a quantum-secure tactical communications network combining PQC and QKD across multiple nodes in a live military exercise in Malta. The trial integrated dynamic key management and software-defined networking, proving that mission-critical data can be protected against both current and future quantum threats without disrupting live operations. The architecture directly counters harvest-now-decrypt-later scenarios.
